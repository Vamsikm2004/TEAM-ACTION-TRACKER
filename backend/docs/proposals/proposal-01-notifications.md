\# Proposal 01: Action Item Notifications and Reminders



Status: Proposed — Awaiting Review



\## 1. Problem Statement



Team members may miss approaching deadlines or overdue action items because the application currently relies on users checking the dashboard.



\## 2. Evidence



The application already supports:

\- Action-item due dates.

\- Assignment to team members.

\- Status tracking.

\- Overdue-action identification.



However, automated notifications and reminders are not currently documented as implemented features.



\## 3. Business Impact



\- Improves deadline awareness.

\- Encourages timely task completion.

\- Reduces manual follow-ups by managers.

\- Improves team accountability.



\## 4. Proposed Solution



Introduce a notification and reminder mechanism that:

\- Identifies approaching and overdue deadlines.

\- Notifies the assigned team member.

\- Avoids repeatedly sending duplicate reminders.

\- Provides a record of notification delivery.



\## 5. Dependencies



\- Existing action-item and user data.

\- Scheduled background processing.

\- Notification delivery mechanism.

\- Notification preferences and delivery configuration.



\## 6. Risks



\- Excessive notifications may reduce user engagement.

\- Email delivery may fail.

\- Incorrect scheduling may trigger duplicate reminders.

\- Additional infrastructure may be required.



\## 7. Expected Outcome



A proactive reminder mechanism that improves visibility and helps teams complete assigned work on time.
